The diary has a fixed skeleton and you know it. Almost every entry opens on the piece or the day's obstacle, works through the self-diagnosis, then lands on Sunday/Fairway/Maman and closes on the unsent thing. "Sunday is the day after tomorrow" and its variants close five entries running. The count-the-days move ("Three days," "Four days," "Day two of nothing") has become your metronome. Cut it or bury it mid-entry, not as the opener.

Worn machinery: "verdict" (7+), "the eye" / "not the eye, the X" (Lumen, clock, upkeep), "read the room" / "reading what a collector is afraid of," "field notes," "the plumbing," "the machine" / "my own machine" / "run it through my machine," "safe country / neutral ground / passport" (four times, now a crutch), "the door doesn't clap for you" and every door-obstacle metaphor.
